About me

Builder. Musician. Researcher.

I'm a senior at Algonquin Regional High School in Southborough, Massachusetts. I founded a non-profit that teaches VEX Robotics, I've studied violin for 13 years, and this year I wrote a machine-learning research paper that was accepted for publication.

I lead the Science National Honor Society, the Science Research Club, and our Science Olympiad team, and I play soccer for the varsity team. This site shows what I've done and what I care about.
